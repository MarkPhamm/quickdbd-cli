# Storylines

Three films for the 0.6.0 redo (dbt yml + a better look at the UI). The first was built.

1. **One real dbt project, from text to PR** (built). After the 200-table hook, github.com/MarkPhamm/qdbd-dbt-demo
   carries the film: its ERD text is built and explored, its yml docs appear on dim_customer, a column typed only in
   the ERD raises a drift warning in VS Code and is fixed in the yml, and the qa branch's dim_cabin lands green in the
   PR diff. Every step causes the next, and 13 tables keep the UI readable on a phone.
2. **Result first.** Open on the finished, documented diagram being explored, then pull back to show it came from a
   short text file plus the yml the team already had.
3. **dbt as the hook.** Open on a yml description being typed and appearing in the diagram a beat later, then widen to
   the rest of qdbd.
